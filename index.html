Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do we have to rebrand?

No. Your firm keeps its name, and invoices go out under your brand. In local IT, the client relationship is with you, and we're not going to disturb that.

Will you make us switch our PSA or RMM?

Never. Our platform connects to ConnectWise, Autotask and HaloPSA on the PSA side and NinjaOne, Datto RMM and N-central on the RMM side. Your PSA stays the system of record. At close, we audit the stack to remove duplicate spend and conflicting automations, but we don't migrate it.

What happens to my team?

Your team is the business. They stay with your firm, and the shared bench takes repetitive work off their plates so they can spend more time on the clients and projects that need them.

How does the stake I keep work?

You keep 30–40% of your own firm. The partnership agreement sets out clearly how that stake is valued and what happens on a future sale. It includes tag-along protection and a path to liquidity after a lock-up period. We write it carefully once, and the same framework applies to every partner.

Will my clients notice anything?

Mostly, they'll notice faster response times and new security and compliance options. The people they call stay the same.

Is this conversation confidential?

Yes. We know how small this industry is. Conversations are confidential from the first call, and we're happy to sign an NDA before anything is shared.
